From sketch to steel

How Fulcrum went from a notebook page to a welded stool in the NIFT workshop.

Ideate

Dozens of quick sketches, chasing a form that is both stable and light.

Draft

15-inch triangles, 14 inches tall. The side-side-side rule locks the shape.

Fabricate

Mild steel square rod, measured, angled and arc welded by hand.

Finish

A plywood top with an olive foam cushion. Seat and side piece in one.

About
Every problem is an opportunity to innovate, and every challenge a chance to grow.

I’m a design student studying Fashion & Lifestyle Accessory Design at NIFT Srinagar. I’m originally from Kerala, and I take inspiration from culture, craftsmanship, materials and everyday life.

I like experimenting with form, texture and colour to make products that are user-centric. Good design should look good and work well.
